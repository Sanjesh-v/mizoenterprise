# MizoEnterprise

### Rural MSME Development and Market Linkage Platform for Mizoram

MizoEnterprise is a proposed research-led initiative to understand
the operational and market-access challenges faced by rural
enterprises and traditional industries in Mizoram.

The project aims to develop a practical framework for improving
enterprise visibility, business diagnostics and market linkages.

## Problem Statement

Rural enterprises may face constraints related to market access,
production capacity, input procurement, infrastructure,
business visibility and access to institutional support.

The nature and extent of these constraints must be established
through field research rather than assumed across all enterprises.

## Initial Research Clusters

- Baktawng: Furniture and carpentry enterprises
- Thenzawl: Handloom and traditional weaving
- Reiek: Turmeric-based processing and enterprises
- Bamboo-based rural enterprises: To be mapped and validated

## Proposed Platform Modules

1. Enterprise Directory
2. Business Diagnostics
3. Market and Buyer Discovery
4. Government Scheme Information
5. Cluster Analytics

## Technology Stack

- Python
- Streamlit
- Pandas
- Plotly
- NumPy

## Research Approach

The project will involve consultations with:

- Rural MSME owners
- Artisans and craftspeople
- Raw material suppliers
- Retailers and wholesalers
- Institutional buyers
- Relevant government officials

## Project Status

Concept development and demonstration MVP.

All included enterprise records and business figures are
synthetic demonstration data, not verified business observations.

## Expected Deliverables

- Rural enterprise database framework
- Business bottleneck analysis
- Market opportunity assessment
- Buyer discovery framework
- Cluster development recommendations
- Functional demonstration prototype

## Objective

To understand the operational challenges of rural enterprises
and identify practical opportunities for improving their
competitiveness, market access and long-term sustainability.
