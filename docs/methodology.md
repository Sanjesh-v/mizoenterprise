# MizoEnterprise - Research Methodology

## Objective

Understand the operational and market-access challenges
of selected rural enterprises in Mizoram.

## Phase 1: Secondary Research

Review:
- New India Internship reports
- Mizoram economic and industrial reports
- MSME policy documents
- Government scheme guidelines
- Industry and market studies

Record sources, publication dates and relevant limitations.

## Phase 2: Primary Research

Proposed exploratory targets:

- 15-20 rural enterprise owners or artisans
- 5-10 retailers, wholesalers or potential buyers
- Consultations with relevant MSME and industry officials

These are proposed research targets, not completed interviews
or statistically representative samples.

## Phase 3: Enterprise Interviews

### Enterprise Owners

- What products do you manufacture?
- How much can you produce monthly?
- What are your major raw materials?
- How do you procure inputs?
- What are your major operating expenses?
- Who are your existing customers?
- What prevents you from reaching new markets?

### Buyers

- What products are you interested in purchasing?
- What quantity and quality do you require?
- What are your procurement criteria?
- What prevents you from sourcing directly from rural enterprises?

### Government Officials

- Which schemes support rural enterprises?
- What are the eligibility requirements?
- What infrastructure support is available?
- What implementation gaps are observed?

## Phase 4: Analysis

- Map enterprise clusters.
- Identify recurring operational constraints.
- Analyse input and sales channels.
- Identify buyer requirements.
- Assess potential shared infrastructure.
- Evaluate relevant institutional support.

## Phase 5: Prototype Validation

Present the prototype to selected stakeholders.
Collect feedback and revise the platform.

## Research Ethics

- Obtain informed consent.
- Avoid collecting unnecessary personal information.
- Anonymise business-sensitive information.
- Do not publish private financial or contact details.
- Distinguish field observations from assumptions.
