# MizoEnterprise - Project Proposal

## Project Title

MizoEnterprise: Rural MSME Development and Market Linkage
Framework for Mizoram.

## Overview

MizoEnterprise is a proposed enterprise-development initiative
focused on understanding the operational and market challenges
faced by rural MSMEs and traditional industries in Mizoram.

It combines enterprise mapping, business diagnostics,
market discovery and institutional support information.

## Proposed Modules

### 1. Enterprise Directory

A structured database of:
- Enterprise name or anonymised identifier
- Village and enterprise cluster
- Industry
- Products
- Production capacity
- Business category

### 2. Business Diagnostics

An assessment framework covering:
- Raw material sourcing
- Production capacity
- Operating costs
- Infrastructure
- Labour requirements
- Packaging and quality
- Sales channels

### 3. Market and Buyer Discovery

Explore potential connections with:
- Retailers
- Wholesalers
- Tourism outlets
- Institutional buyers
- Distributors

Buyer requirements must be validated before recommendations
are made.

### 4. Scheme Information

Organise publicly available information about relevant
government schemes, eligibility and application procedures.

Scheme eligibility and current availability must be verified
with the respective authorities.

### 5. Cluster Analytics

Explore:
- Enterprise distribution
- Industry concentration
- Production capacity
- Market access requirements
- Shared infrastructure opportunities

## Technology

- Python
- Streamlit
- Pandas
- Plotly
- CSV datasets

## Expected Deliverables

1. Enterprise mapping framework
2. Business diagnostics
3. Market opportunity assessment
4. Buyer linkage framework
5. Cluster development recommendations
6. Functional demonstration prototype

## Limitations

The prototype is a conceptual demonstration.
Its features must be refined after consultations with
actual enterprises and other stakeholders.
