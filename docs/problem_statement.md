# MizoEnterprise - Problem Statement

## Background

Mizoram has several traditional and emerging rural enterprises,
including furniture and carpentry, handloom, food processing
and bamboo-based activities.

These enterprises can contribute to rural employment,
local value addition and economic development.

## Problem Being Investigated

The project will investigate whether selected rural enterprises
face challenges relating to:

1. Limited access to wider markets.
2. Input procurement and operating costs.
3. Production capacity and infrastructure.
4. Product quality, packaging and branding.
5. Business visibility and buyer discovery.
6. Awareness and accessibility of government support.

These are research hypotheses, not established findings
for every enterprise or village.

## Initial Clusters

- Baktawng: Furniture and carpentry
- Thenzawl: Handloom
- Reiek: Turmeric processing
- Bamboo-based enterprises: To be identified through research

## Research Questions

- What products do rural enterprises manufacture?
- What are their production capacities?
- What are their major operating expenses?
- How do they currently source raw materials?
- Who are their existing customers?
- What prevents them from reaching new markets?
- Which government support programmes are accessible?

## Objective

Develop an evidence-based framework to improve rural
enterprise visibility, market access and business development.

## Intended Outcome

A field-informed enterprise development prototype and
practical implementation roadmap.
